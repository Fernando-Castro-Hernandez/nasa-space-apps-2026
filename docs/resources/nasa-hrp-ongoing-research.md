# Ongoing HRP Research (NASA HRP)

- **Source:** https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/
- **Page last updated:** 2026-08-18
- **Extracted:** 2026-11-14. Descriptions are paraphrased from the page.

**Purpose:** Lists HRP studies on human health and performance, grouped by mission. HRP uses research to develop methods that protect astronaut health and performance. The full list is on one page (21 studies, no pagination). The page does not tag studies by health area.

## Artemis II (ongoing)

| Study                        | What it does                                                                  | URL                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ARCHeR                       | Tracks crew well-being, activity and sleep in deep space.                     | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/archer/                       |
| Immune Biomarkers            | Analyzes blood and saliva to see how deep space changes the immune system.    | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/immune-biomarkers/            |
| Artemis II Standard Measures | Crew supplies a consistent set of health data to a data bank for researchers. | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/artemis-ii-standard-measures/ |

## Low Earth Orbit (ongoing)

| Study                                        | What it does                                                                           | URL                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| B-Complex                                    | Tests whether a daily B vitamin supplement prevents or lessens brain and eye changes.  | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/b-complex/                                    |
| Brain Fluid Pressure                         | Links fluid pressure inside the skull to eye and brain changes.                        | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/brain-fluid-pressure/                         |
| CIPHER                                       | Suite of studies on how multiple body systems change in orbit.                         | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/cipher/                                       |
| Simulating Manual Piloting for Moon Landings | How gravity transitions affect piloting ability.                                       | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/simulating-manual-piloting-for-moon-landings/ |
| Spacecraft Occupant Protection               | Surveys crew discomfort during re-entry and combines it with spacecraft sensor data.   | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/spacecraft-occupant-protection/               |
| Spinal Fluid Biomarkers                      | Detects spinal fluid substances linked to brain and eye changes.                       | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/spinal-fluid-biomarkers/                      |
| Standard Measures                            | Consistent set of physiological and psychological measurements of crew.                | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/standard-measures/                            |
| Thigh Cuff                                   | Tests whether thigh cuffs keep fluid in the legs, against space-related eye disorders. | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/thigh-cuff/                                   |
| Venous Haemostasis                           | Physical differences that may change blood flow and clotting in space.                 | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/venous-haemostasis/                           |
| Zero T2                                      | Effects of exercising in space without a treadmill.                                    | https://www.nasa.gov/reference/zero-t2/                                      |

## Past investigations

| Study              | What it did                                                                      | URL                                                                                               |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
