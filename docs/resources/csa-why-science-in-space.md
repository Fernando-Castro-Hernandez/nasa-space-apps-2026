**Purpose:** Explains why Canada runs experiments on the ISS: to prepare for deep-space destinations and to use that knowledge to improve life on Earth. "On average, there are 200 international space experiments on board the ISS at any given time."

## Preparing for deep space

- Goal: understand the risks of human spaceflight, find countermeasures and treatments, and "identify, characterize, and mitigate" effects on astronaut health "in order to make space travel safer."
- Moon and Mars missions bring longer trips and more risks:
  - more time in weightlessness
  - more exposure to radiation
  - longer isolation, very far from home
  - longer communication delays with Earth
  - less ability to rely on Earth for food, materials and emergency medical help

## Benefits on Earth

- "The effects observed in astronauts are similar to accelerated aging and health problems caused by a sedentary lifestyle."
- "Studying the human body in space for six months gives us data that would take years to gather on Earth."
- Canadian scientists study bones, heart, blood vessels and brain in space. Findings can help people with balance problems (such as seniors), osteoporosis, cardiovascular disorders and type 2 diabetes.
